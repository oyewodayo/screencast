The video editor's biggest release yet: blend modes, adjustment layers and motion tracking, exports that render in the background while you keep editing, and sharing straight to YouTube or a link. There's a free music and sound-effects library, animated elements, title reveals, highlight intros, split screens, new transitions with live previews, and exports up to 11 times faster. The PDF viewer gets a cleaner layout with fit-to-screen and book-style page turns, and every library file can be moved or copied to any folder in a couple of keystrokes.

### Blend modes

- Text, pictures, elements and picture-in-picture videos can **blend** with the video under them: Multiply, Screen, Overlay, Hard light, Darken, Lighten, Difference, Exclusion and Add. Drop light leaks, film grain or dust footage over a video with **Screen**, or lay a scanned signature on with **Multiply**.
- The export uses the same formula as the preview, checked against the standard on every pair of colour values.

### Adjustment layers

- **Add adjustment layer** (on the timeline toolbar) grades or blurs everything beneath it for a stretch of the video: a look (Vibrant, Film, Warm, Cool, B&W), brightness, contrast, saturation, hue, blur and vignette, with a mix amount and fade in/out.
- Choose what it covers: **the video** (titles and pictures stay crisp) or **everything** (only captions stay on top).
- Limit it to a rectangle or ellipse with a soft edge, or flip it to **everything except the area**, to blur or darken the background around a speaker.

### Motion tracking

- **Track motion…** pins a title, sticker or picture to something moving: box what it should follow and Briefcast follows it through the clip, its size too if you like. It finds the object again after it's briefly hidden.
- **Blur regions** can follow a face or a number plate the same way.
- Drag a tracked overlay to move it along its whole path. The motion plays identically in the preview and the exported video.
- Recognises cuts within a clip, and keeps a slowly drifting box on the object's own appearance.

### Background exports

- **Save** now queues the export and lets you keep editing straight away. The exports chip shows the frame being rendered, how far it's got and how long is left. Export several versions in a row, cancel one, or retry one that failed.
- Exports run at a lower priority, so the editor stays smooth while they render. Closing Briefcast mid-export asks first.

### Faster exports

- Exports are up to **11 times faster**: Briefcast reads only the parts of a recording it uses, encodes on your graphics card where that's proven to give the same picture, and decodes on it where that's measurably quicker.
- **Plain cuts keep the original picture**: when you've only trimmed and cut, the untouched parts are copied as they are and only the frames at each cut are re-encoded. A 41-second cut from a 10-minute recording saves in about 4 seconds.
- The progress bar now measures the video you're exporting, not the whole recording it came from.

### Chapters and sharing

- **Chapters** (on the timeline toolbar): add them at the playhead, take the markers you placed in the player, or let Briefcast suggest them from the transcript, titled from what's said. They stay on their shot as you edit, and they're saved into the exported file.
- **Share** a finished export from the exports panel:
  - **YouTube**: title, description (chapters added as timestamps, so YouTube shows them as chapters), tags, category and visibility, with your captions attached as subtitles. Uploads resume after a dropped connection.
  - **Share link**: the video on your own Google Drive, with a link anyone can watch from.
  - **Send**: the Windows share window, or copy the file to paste anywhere.

### Music and sound effects

- **Music** opens a free library of songs and sound effects: browse or search, preview instantly, see **Suggested for this video**, and add the best part of a song in one click. Every track is free to use; the few that ask for a credit give you the line to paste.
- **Lower under speech** (on by default for library music over a talking video) dips the music whenever someone speaks, and brings it back up in the gaps, the same in the preview and the export.
- Audio tracks can be **split at the playhead**.

### Elements, titles and intros

- **Elements**: 40 shapes and animated stickers to drop on a video: arrows, a hand-drawn circle and highlighter, speech bubbles, a subscribe button, lower thirds, badges, location pins, click and swipe markers, countdowns and timers, a REC light, confetti, sparkles, an equalizer, a progress bar, cinema bars and more. Each has its own entrance, exit, loop and colours.
- **Title reveals**: text can type itself out letter by letter, appear word by word, or rise into place, with a caret and an accent underline if you like.
- **Picture reveals**: wipe, glare, focus pull or fade a picture in, with an optional repeating shine.
- **Highlight intro**: a fast montage of your best moments before the video starts, cut on the music's beat or freely, with light leak, flash, whip or bar-open transitions.
- **Split screen**: 2 or 3 stacked, side by side, 3 columns, a grid, or one feature with 2 or 3 beside it, built in one step. Each shot can be a video or a picture (cropped to fill its cell), chosen from the timeline, picked from disk, or dragged in from the sidebar.
- Resize pictures, elements and blur regions from any edge or corner.

### Transitions and camera motion

- Transitions now play **both clips moving**: the outgoing clip keeps playing through the transition instead of freezing on its last frame.
- New **Page curl**, **Zoom blur** and **Colour sweep** transitions, and light leaks, flashes and colour sweeps take your colour, direction and size.
- **16 camera motions** on any clip: zoom in and out, smooth push, punch in, bounce in, pans in four directions, drift, sway, float, breathe, pulse, shake and vibrate.
- Transitions, colour looks and camera motions are chosen from **live previews on your own footage**.
- Every transition, look and camera motion exports exactly as it previews.

### The editor

- **Clip effects** are organised into tabs: Look, Motion, Sound and Transition, with a dot on each tab in use.
- **Ctrl+K** searches every tool on the toolbar.
- **Select several clips** (Ctrl- or Shift-click, or Ctrl+A) to delete, duplicate or change them together, and **merge** back-to-back clips into one.
- Clip speed now goes up to **16×**.
- **Drag videos, pictures and sound from the sidebar** onto the timeline to insert them, or onto the video to put them right there (a picture as an overlay, a video as picture-in-picture). Or use the **+** next to any file: insert at the playhead, add to the end, or show it on the video.
- **Copy and paste** on the timeline: **Ctrl+C**, **Ctrl+X** and **Ctrl+V** for clips, titles, pictures, blur regions, picture-in-picture, sound and adjustment layers, pasted at the playhead. Files copied in File Explorer paste straight in too, and so does a screenshot or a picture copied from a browser (saved to a **Pasted** folder).
- Playback moves from one clip to the next without a gap.
- Dragging a slider is one undo step, not one per movement.
- A title, picture or blur region added with the playhead at the very end of the video now gets its full length instead of a few milliseconds.

### Thumbnails and video files

- **Set thumbnail** lets you pick any frame (scrub, step frame by frame, or choose from a strip of candidates) or a picture, and dress it up with a title, subtitle, caption bar, badge or logo. It's embedded in MP4 files, and **Also make a share-ready copy** makes a copy that opens on that thumbnail in messaging apps.
- Videos the built-in player can't show, like iPhone HEVC .MOV files and .avi recordings, play from a compatible copy made automatically.

### Library

- **Move or copy to…** files, or a whole selection, to any library folder from the sidebar, the galleries and the editor. Type to find a folder, or create one right there; your recent destinations are at the top; it all works from the keyboard.
- Renaming, moving, copying or deleting a file now takes its saved edits, chapters and annotations with it.

### PDF viewer

- PDFs open **fitted to the screen** and stay fitted as you resize the window. Turn it off in **Settings** to open at your default zoom.
- In two-page view, pages **turn like a book**, or instantly if you prefer (Settings → **Two-page turn**). Swipe with two fingers on a trackpad, or one finger on a touch screen, to turn pages.
- A cleaner layout: the page has the whole window, with zoom at the top right, and the **Edit** button and page navigator at the bottom right. **Edit** opens the markup tools beside the page.
- **Select ink and highlights** to move or delete them.

### Recording

- The voice now lines up with the picture exactly, including when you record a video playing on screen: system sound is placed at the moment it's heard, which also takes Bluetooth headphones' delay into account.

### Fixes

- A blur region in the exported video is now exactly as strong as in the preview. It used to come out much weaker, which could leave a face or text readable.
- A picture-in-picture video that starts partway through the timeline now plays correctly in the export. It used to show the wrong part or freeze.
- A Ken Burns zoom now moves in the exported video. It used to stay at one size.
- Pop-in titles now animate in the export, and colour looks match the preview exactly.
- Text now stays above pictures in the export, as it does in the preview.
- The clip vignette look no longer darkens titles and pictures in the preview, matching the export.
