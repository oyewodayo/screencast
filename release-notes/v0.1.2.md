Docs grows into a full word processor in this release: headers, footers and page numbers, watermarks, templates, one-step emailing and sharing, and Word, Markdown and text files that open straight into Docs. The video editor can now edit a video by editing its transcript, make videos from your photos, burn styled captions into a video, and preview transitions live. Recordings also survive a screen capture that drops out mid-recording.

### Email and share documents

- **Export → Email this file** or **Share this file** sends a document as PDF, Word, web page, Markdown or text without saving it anywhere first.
- **Outlook and Thunderbird** open a new email with the file already attached. **Gmail, Outlook.com and Yahoo Mail** open with the recipients, subject and message filled in, and the file ready to drag in or paste.
- **Share with an app** opens the Windows share window (Nearby Share, WhatsApp, Teams, OneDrive and more). There are also one-click **WhatsApp, Telegram, Teams, Slack and Discord**, **Copy file**, and **Copy contents** to paste the document, formatting and all, into an email or chat.

### Headers, footers and page numbers

- Headers and footers now show on every page as you write, exactly where they print. **Double-click a page's top or bottom margin** to edit them.
- Left, centre and right text, with page number, page count, title and date fields.
- **Page numbers** in six positions and five wordings ("Page 1 of 9", "– 1 –"…), in 1 2 3, i ii iii, I II III, a b c or A B C, starting from any number.
- **Different first page** for a title page. Everything prints, goes into PDFs, and becomes real Word headers and footers with live page numbers.

### Watermarks

- **More → Watermark** puts DRAFT, CONFIDENTIAL or your own words (diagonal or horizontal, any colour and size), or a logo or seal, faintly behind the text of every page.
- Optionally leave it off the cover page. It prints, and goes into PDF and Word exports.

### Templates and page setup

- **Start a new document** from 13 templates: research paper, two-column journal letter, thesis, lab notebook, literature review, lecture notes, business report, meeting notes, project proposal, weekly update, invoice, resume/CV and cover letter.
- Page setup adds **landscape pages** and **page colour**, which now covers the whole sheet in print and PDF.

### Papers: equations, columns, captions, citations and contents

- **LaTeX equations**, inline or displayed and numbered (1), (2)…, that export as real Word equations.
- **Two-column layout** and **Document style**, with ready-made styles (APS/RevTeX, IEEE, LaTeX article…), plus a library of bundled fonts (Latin Modern, STIX Two, Libertinus and more) so documents look the same on every computer. The **?** button walks you through writing a two-column paper.
- Numbered **figure and table captions**, and **cross-references** that always show the current number.
- **Citations and a reference list** in Nature, APS, IEEE or APA style, from a DOI, arXiv ID or BibTeX.
- A **table of contents** and a document **outline** panel.
- **Import PDF** rebuilds a paper as an editable document, or keeps its pages exactly as printed.

### Open Word, Markdown and text files in Docs

- .docx, .md and .txt files now open in Docs from anywhere: **Open a file**, the library or Import. The original file is never changed.
- Opening the same file again takes you back to your document, unless the file has changed since.

### Edit video by editing its transcript

- **Edit by transcript** shows everything said in a video as text. Delete words and they're cut from the video; the speech joins up naturally.
- One click removes **filler words** ("um", "uh"), **stutters** and **long pauses**. Hover first to see exactly what goes, and how much time each saves.
- Transcription runs offline on your computer. Words are now timed against the actual speech, so every cut lands where the word really is.

### Make videos from images

- **Create video from images** on the Video Editor screen turns photos into a video: pick them from your library or your computer, choose a 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:3 frame and how long each image shows, and add slow zoom-and-pan movement and crossfades in one go. Or start with a blank timeline.
- Every editor tool works on photos: transitions, color, crop, text, music, captions. Mixed sizes and orientations are fitted into the frame without stretching, iPhone HEIC photos are supported, and phone photos always appear the right way up.
- Projects are kept in your library to reopen and keep editing. **Save** makes a normal `.mp4`.
- Images can also be added to any recording's timeline. Click the new **+** after the last clip, or drop several files at once.

### Burned-in captions

- **Captions** in the video editor writes captions from the speech, offline and on your computer, and burns them into the exported video, so they show anywhere it's played.
- 15 caption styles, from a clean subtitle to word-by-word highlights, with position, size, words per line, word emphasis and ALL CAPS.
- Fix any line by editing its text, and copy or download them all as a transcript. Captions follow your cuts and reordering.
- The player's own captions can now be styled the same way, and caption generation picks up where it stopped if Briefcast is closed mid-way.

### Color grading

- **Effects → Color grading**: exposure, contrast, saturation, temperature, tint, highlights and shadows, with a live RGB histogram and hold-to-compare.
- **Import .cube LUTs**, such as camera log conversions, film looks or grades from other apps, with an amount slider.
- The preview is exactly the export: both apply the same color table, now drawn on the GPU while you edit.

### Video editor

- **Transitions now play live in the preview**, exactly as they'll export. Each one is labelled under its cut on the timeline.
- Transitions no longer shorten the video. They play over the start of the next clip, so text, images and captions stay where you put them in the export.
- **Right-click a clip** for a menu: view it on its own, save just that clip, split, duplicate, move, and set its transition, motion, color and length.
- **Speed ramps**: slow-mo moments, speed-ups and slow-downs across a clip, playing live and exporting to the exact length.
- **Freeze frame** and **Reverse** (every frame kept) in the clip menu, and **Stabilize** to remove camera shake.
- **Motion keyframes** for text and images: move, scale and fade them over time, with smooth, eased or held moves.
- **Cut to the beat**: find the tempo and beats of a music track, snap to them, and time a slideshow's images to the beat.
- **Export for any platform**: 16:9, 9:16, 1:1 or 4:5, fitted with a blurred background or bars, or filled. **Follow the subject** moves the crop with the action, like a camera operator, and the player shows the kept area before you export.
- **Loudness targets**: -14 LUFS for streaming or -16 LUFS for podcasts, hit within 0.1 LU with peaks kept below -2 dBFS.
- Crop, Ken Burns and mirror now preview exactly as they export, including clips of a different shape. Splitting a clip keeps all of its settings, and a speed ramp or Ken Burns move carries on across the pieces.

### Home screen

- **From your library** now lists documents, whiteboards and mind maps alongside your recordings, most recent first.

### Also new

- If the window reloads, for example after waking the laptop from sleep, Briefcast reopens what you were working on instead of the home screen.
- **Settings → Appearance → Show status bar** hides the bottom row (date and time, memory use, feedback link).
- Phone photo thumbnails in the library now appear the right way up.

### Recording

- If the screen capture stops mid-recording (a display change, a UAC prompt, a driver reset), Briefcast restarts it within a second or two while the audio carries on. The recording bar shows **reconnecting** and then **recovered**, and the finished recording is complete.
