A cleanup release: fixes for what broke or got in the way in v0.1.3, smoother round trips between files, and less work running in the background while you edit.

### Fixes

- **Sound going silent after opening the Mixer.** If Windows had switched or woken the speakers since Briefcast started (headphones or a Bluetooth set connected, a monitor's speakers asleep, the PC back from sleep), opening the Mixer could silence every sound in the editor while the meters and waveforms kept moving. Briefcast now reconnects to whatever output Windows is using the moment any of those happens.
- **Split screens showing a black square where a video should be.** The gap colour was drawn over every video shot. It now sits beneath them, and split screens made before are repaired when the video is opened.
- **A split screen's speaker out of step with the sound.** The shot taken from the clip under the playhead started from that clip's beginning; it now plays from the playhead, in step with the main video.
- **Handles staying on the picture.** The handles around a selected picture, blur region or picture-in-picture now step aside while the video plays, and clicking the black area around the picture lets go of it.
- **Headers and footers in the middle of a document.** When a long table ran past the end of a page, the header and footer bands further down could land on top of the text, with a dashed outline that blocked clicks and selection. They now stay in the page margins. Watermarks and letterheads follow the real pages the same way.

### Smoother editing

- **Coming back to a video puts it back as you left it.** Open a linked document (or a folder, or another file) and return: the editor is open again if it was open, closed if it was closed, and the playhead is where you left it.
- **The picture sits above the player controls while editing**, instead of under them behind a dark shade, so the whole frame stays visible as you work.
- **E** opens and closes the editor while a video is open (listed in the player's **?** shortcuts).
- **AI voice-overs can be found and re-made.** The AI voice tab lists the voice-overs you've made, this video's first in timeline order. Click one to bring back its words, voice and pace; change a word and **Replace voice-over** re-makes it in the same place. **+** places one again at the playhead instantly. Voice-overs made with v0.1.3 or earlier show the start of their words. Paste the full text and Replace still puts the new one in the same spot.
- **Make room where you need it.** Drag the sidebar's edge to widen it (up to 2.2× its usual width): names show in full as it grows, thumbnails add columns, and the mixer and AI voice panels spread out. It keeps one width for watching and another for editing, and moves between them as the editor opens and closes. Drag it all the way left, or press **Ctrl+\\**, to fold it into a strip of icons for the most picture.
- **Timeline height.** Drag the top edge of the timeline up for more lanes or down for more picture; double-click it for automatic.
- **The pointer hand keeps up.** The hand and the ink follow the cursor without lag, circles draw smoothly, and the hand no longer swings on its stick.
- **Split screen, rebuilt around a live preview.** New **Top & bottom** (you under your media, the way vertical commentary videos are made) and **Card on top** (a soft-edged clip floating over you) layouts. Drop shots straight onto the preview, drag to frame them, scroll to zoom, drag the divider (it snaps to thirds and half). Every video shot starts framed on its subject. Change it any time afterwards: select one of its layers for a bar over the picture with every layout, swap and divider positions, or **Edit** to reopen it. A split screen also moves and trims as one on the timeline.
- **Made for phones.** The new **Canvas** button sets the shape you edit in and save at: TikTok · Reels · Shorts, Instagram post, square, YouTube, or a phone's whole screen (iPhone 17, 17 Pro Max, Air, Galaxy S25, Pixel 10). The player shows exactly that shape; every layer keeps its place and split screens re-flow to fill it. A landscape clip sits over a blurred copy of itself instead of black bars, and **Phone preview** shows the screen's corners and where an app's buttons and caption will cover your video.
- **A Video Editor home that gets you going.** **Continue editing** with your last edit as it now stands, **Resume** and **Send export to phone**; starters for a vertical video, a split screen or Shorts that open the editor already set up; a library with lengths, Edited/Export/Vertical badges, filters, sorting, a moving preview on hover, and each video's exports listed on its own card. Drop a video file on it to start editing.
- **Easier to find your work.** New projects go into a **Projects** folder with a proper name instead of "Untitled video (38)", and exports into an **Exports** folder numbered by version (`My video - v2.mp4`) instead of "(edited) (edited)". The small files that hold your edits are hidden in File Explorer (they still travel with their file). **Tidy up library** moves earlier projects and exports into their folders and clears out the saved edits of files that no longer exist, showing everything first and moving nothing until you say so.
- **A search hiding folders now says so.** The sidebar shows how many folders the search is leaving out, with **Show all**. **Esc** in the search box clears it.

### Performance

- Voice-overs, music and picture-in-picture sounds that were deleted, re-made or auditioned in the Mixer no longer keep processing audio in the background for the rest of the session.
- **The sidebar answers at once.** Clicking a file or folder lights it up immediately, and folder arrows open and close instantly however big the library. The file list no longer redraws itself several times a second while a video plays or the editor is busy.
