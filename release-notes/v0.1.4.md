A cleanup release: fixes for what broke or got in the way in v0.1.3, smoother round trips between files, and less work running in the background while you edit.

### Fixes

- **Sound going silent after opening the Mixer.** If Windows had switched or woken the speakers since Briefcast started (headphones or a Bluetooth set connected, a monitor's speakers asleep, the PC back from sleep), opening the Mixer could silence every sound in the editor while the meters and waveforms kept moving. Briefcast now reconnects to whatever output Windows is using the moment any of those happens.
- **Headers and footers in the middle of a document.** When a long table ran past the end of a page, the header and footer bands further down could land on top of the text, with a dashed outline that blocked clicks and selection. They now stay in the page margins. Watermarks and letterheads follow the real pages the same way.

### Smoother editing

- **Coming back to a video puts it back as you left it.** Open a linked document (or a folder, or another file) and return: the editor is open again if it was open, closed if it was closed, and the playhead is where you left it.
- **The picture sits above the player controls while editing**, instead of under them behind a dark shade, so the whole frame stays visible as you work.
- **E** opens and closes the editor while a video is open (listed in the player's **?** shortcuts).
- **AI voice-overs can be found and re-made.** The AI voice tab lists the voice-overs you've made, this video's first in timeline order. Click one to bring back its words, voice and pace; change a word and **Replace voice-over** re-makes it in the same place. **+** places one again at the playhead instantly. Voice-overs made with v0.1.3 or earlier show the start of their words. Paste the full text and Replace still puts the new one in the same spot.
- **A search hiding folders now says so.** The sidebar shows how many folders the search is leaving out, with **Show all**. **Esc** in the search box clears it.

### Performance

- Voice-overs, music and picture-in-picture sounds that were deleted, re-made or auditioned in the Mixer no longer keep processing audio in the background for the rest of the session.
