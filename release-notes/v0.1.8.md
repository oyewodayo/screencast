Briefcast v0.1.8 introduces **1-Click Studio Polish**, a **Post-Recording Action Strip**, a full **Video → Briefcast Doc → Publish to Briefs** creator loop, a crystal-clear separation between **Watch mode** and **Edit mode**, dramatically faster & cancellable offline Whisper transcription with smooth live progress, zero-setup bundled universal `ffmpeg` on macOS, guided first-run onboarding, and a major Rust & React modularization pass for better performance and maintainability.

### 1-Click Studio Polish & Post-Recording Action Strip

- **1-Click Studio Polish**: A new **Polish** button in the Video Editor toolbar bundles Briefcast's separate finishing tools into one toggleable pass — **Framed screen backdrop**, **Pro voice audio** (RNNoise voice isolation, Clear Voice EQ, Podcast compressor, peak limiter & `-14 LUFS` loudness), **Trim dead air**, **Auto-zoom on clicks**, and **Cursor ripples & keystroke chips** — with live per-step results and a direct **Turn into Doc** follow-up action.
- **Live Real-Time Backdrop Preview, 18 Studio Gradients, Custom Color & Image**: Clicking any of the **18 curated studio gradients** (including 6 new themes: `Cobalt`, `Velvet`, `Obsidian`, `Ember`, `Glacial`, `Sage`) in the horizontally scrollable swatch strip, picking any custom solid **Color**, choosing a custom **Image…** backdrop, or clicking **Remove** updates the video preview instantaneously in real time — with a **Fine-tune…** shortcut to jump straight into Screen Style's full padding, corner radius, and shadow sliders.
- **Dockable into the Left Sidebar Tools Tab**: Studio Polish docks smoothly into the left sidebar's **Tools** tab whenever the sidebar is open and floats cleanly as a popover when it is closed.
- **Post-Recording Quick Action Strip**: Upgraded the recording-completed popup with a 2×2 **Next steps** strip — **Studio Polish**, **Add Captions**, **Create Doc**, and **Convert** — right above **Close / Play**, so you can polish, transcribe, or write linked notes the instant you hit Stop.

### Briefcast → Briefs Creator Loop (Video → Doc & Publish to Briefs)

- **Turn Video into Briefcast Doc (Editor & Watch Mode)**: Convert any recording, tutorial, or edited video into a structured, illustrated, back-linked **Briefcast Doc** directly from the Watch-mode player bar (**Doc** button) or inside the Editor from Studio Polish, Captions, Chapters, or Share:
  - Automatically transcribes speech offline when no transcript exists yet (saving the result straight back onto the video timeline), with a live progress bar, **Stop & Cancel**, and a **Skip transcription & build visual doc now** option.
  - Resolves chapters from timeline markers, player `.chapters.json` bookmarks, or offline TextTiling chapter detection.
  - Extracts a local offline **Summary** and timestamped **Key Takeaways** directly from the transcript, embeds **chapter screenshots** captured into the doc's own asset folder, and formats the full transcript into clean readable paragraphs under each chapter.
- **Publish to Briefs**:
  - **Briefcast Docs**: Added a 1-click **Publish to Briefs** card in the Share dialog that copies formatted HTML + plain text to your clipboard and opens `withbriefs.com/brief/create`.
  - **Exported Videos**: Added a dedicated **Briefs** tab in the Video Share panel that auto-fills watch links from YouTube or Google Drive, previews the formatted post with optional chapters & transcript, and offers both **Create Briefcast Doc** and **Publish to Briefs**.

### Clear Watch Mode vs. Edit Mode Distinction

- **No more false "Continue editing" on watched videos**: Fixed an edit-store bug where simply opening a video to watch wrote an untouched `.edits.json` sidecar on close, causing watched tutorials to appear under **Continue editing** and the **Edited** filter in Video Editor Home. Added strict real-edit detection (`hasVideoEdits`) so existing untouched sidecars are ignored automatically.
- **Unmistakable Watch ↔ Edit controls**: Added a clear **`Edit` / `Editing`** pill (`E`) in the video player bar, an **`EDITOR`** badge + 1-click **`Watch`** exit button (and `File → Switch to Watch Mode`) in the Editor menu bar, synced dock icon highlights, and a quick-**Watch** hover button on Video Editor Home tiles.

### Faster, Smoother & Cancellable Offline Transcription

- **Single-flight worker management, Cancel button & quit cleanup**: Added a single-flight lock and `cancel_transcription` command so multiple `whisper-cli` workers can never pile up and thrash CPU, added a live **Cancel** button inside the Captions and Transcript Editor panels as well as the Video-to-Doc modal, and wired automatic `whisper-cli` termination on app exit (`RunEvent::Exit`).
- **3–5× faster CPU decoding & live progress**: Switched to 90-second resumable chunks, fast greedy decoding (`-bs 1 -bo 1`), UI-friendly thread headroom, and continuous `stdout` timestamp + `\r`-aware `stderr` progress parsing so the progress bar moves smoothly instead of stalling at `0%`.

### Cross-Platform Polish, First-Run Onboarding & Architecture

- **Zero-setup macOS `ffmpeg` bundling & window monitoring**: Bundled universal (`arm64` + `x86_64`) static `ffmpeg` and `ffprobe` binaries directly inside macOS release builds so recording and export work out of the box without installing Homebrew, paired with native macOS window focus monitoring and a guided **FfmpegSetupBanner** copy-command + recheck card when `ffmpeg` is missing on Linux/dev machines.
- **First-run Quick Start strip**: Added a dismissible **Home Quick Start** onboarding card for new libraries (`< 3` items) to launch Record, Edit, Docs, Whiteboard, or Design in one click.
- **Major Modularization & Test Coverage**: Split `conversion.rs` (12,606 lines) into 7 focused Rust modules, extracted `recording/sidecars.rs` and `recording/filters.rs`, split `WhiteboardStylePanel.tsx` (−46%) into 5 dedicated inspector modules, extracted `TimelineTrackWidgets.tsx`, `timelineHelpers.ts`, `useTimelineOverlayDrags.ts`, `useTimelinePlaybackSync.ts`, `HomeWorkspace.tsx`, and 4 cohesive Dashboard hooks (`useAnnotationController`, `useMediaAssetResolvers`, `useDashboardSystemListeners`, `useHomeCreationIndexes`), backed by expanded Vitest suites.

### First-Time macOS Install Note

Because Briefcast isn't Apple-notarized yet, macOS may show *"Apple could not verify 'Briefcast' is free of malware"* on first launch. After dragging **Briefcast** into **Applications**, open it once using either option:

1. **System Settings (recommended):** Open Briefcast once and click **Done** → open **System Settings → Privacy & Security** → scroll to **Security** → click **Open Anyway** next to Briefcast and confirm with your password or Touch ID.
2. **Terminal:** Run `xattr -cr /Applications/Briefcast.app` and then open Briefcast normally.
