# Briefcast documentation

Briefcast is a free, open-source desktop app for recording, editing and explaining your work. It records your screen, webcam and microphone, gives you a video editor with offline captions, and keeps docs, whiteboards, mindmaps, image boards and annotated PDFs next to the recordings they explain. Everything stays on your computer.

- **[Board](board.md)**: compose several images into one layout and export it as a single picture.
